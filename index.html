Parry Lord

One duel, two swords. Tap parry just before a blow lands to deflect it and rattle your rival's posture. Break their posture or drain their health. First to 4 rounds wins the match.

Keyboard: move with WASD or arrows, attack with J or left click, parry with K, Space or right click (tap to deflect, hold to guard). Touch: left side to move, buttons on the right.

Rotate your phone sideways for a bigger arena
